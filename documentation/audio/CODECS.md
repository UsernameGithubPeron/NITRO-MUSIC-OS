# NITRO N62 Audio Codec Inventory

Device: Maxwest NITRO N62
Platform: MediaTek MT6739
Android: 12

## Configured audio decoders

- MP3 — Google and MediaTek
- AAC
- FLAC
- Opus
- Vorbis
- AMR-NB
- AMR-WB
- GSM
- G.711 A-law
- G.711 mu-law

## Configured audio encoders

- AAC
- AMR-NB
- AMR-WB
- FLAC

## Source files

- media_codecs_google_audio.xml
- media_codecs_mediatek_audio.xml

## Notes

These formats are declared in the stock codec configuration.
Actual playback and encoding support must be tested on the device.
Raw audio (audio/raw) is also declared, but is not a compressed
music format.
